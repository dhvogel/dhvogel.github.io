2Y Breaks Record For Participation Points

Chad Ryerson averaged 847 hand-raises per semester on his way to a record 125 participation points, shattering the previous benchmark set during the Zoom era when cameras could remain off.

By Dan Vogel, Campus Correspondent  |  June 21, 2026

NEW HAVEN — There’s a new sheriff in town. Chad Ryerson, a second-year student from Pelican Rapids, Minnesota, has broken the Yale SOM record for most participation points earned in a single MBA, finishing with a perfect five out of five in every Spring 2 course.

Ryerson graduates with 125 participation points total, one point ahead of prior record holder Tyler Stevenson, whose 124-point run was derailed by a single four out of five in Spring 2 Innovator—an event the community now refers to simply as “The Stevenson Moment.”

“We all knew about the Stevenson Moment,” Ryerson told SOM Observer. “At the end of first year I looked at my participation point total and I said to myself, ‘you know, Chad, it might just be possible.’ And it was.”

Yale SOM Admits Model T-97-A to Class of 2028

In two years’ time, T-97-A will be a robot with a business school education. Administrators call it the future of business; students worry about the grade curve.

By Dan Vogel, Careers Correspondent  |  June 21, 2026

Model T-97-A is not just any robot. The Yale School of Management has offered admission to the Class of 2028, marking the first time a non-human applicant has been accepted to the MBA program.

“We are thrilled to have T-97-A as a part of the class of 2028,” said Dean of Students Kevin Coughlan. “We think this is the future of business.” Career Development Officer Christine Frank cited skyrocketing demand for robots in banking, consulting, and software engineering.

Not everyone is celebrating. “What’s going to happen to the grade curve?” asked second-year student James Smith. “How are we going to compete with a robot that never turns off? Morgan Stanley forces me to go home at 1AM. They don’t force the robot to go home.”
