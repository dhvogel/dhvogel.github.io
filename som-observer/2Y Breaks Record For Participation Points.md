There’s a new sheriff in town. Chad Ryerson, 1Y from Pelican Rapids, MN, has just broken the Yale SOM record for most participation points in a single year. With a full five out of five participation points in all of his Spring 2 courses, Chad will be graduating with a grand total of one hundred and twenty five participation points throughout his two years at Yale SOM, edging out prior record holder Tyler Stevenson, who had the prior record of one hundred and twenty four, after inexplicably earning only a 4 out of 5 in Spring 2 Innovator, despite being one of the top contributors in the class, in what has been termed “The Stevenson Moment”. When asked how he was able to achieve this feat, Chad said, “You know, I never intended to come here and break the record. Of course, I knew about the Stevenson Moment. We all knew about the Stevenson Moment. But at the end of first year I looked at my participation point total and I said to myself, ‘you know, Chad, it might just be possible. And it was.’” And it was.
