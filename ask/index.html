Ask about Dan

A small AI model answers questions about Dan using a knowledge graph of his work, projects, and writing. The model runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.

Starting downloads a one-time ~0.9 GB AI model to your browser, which then runs locally and is cached for next time. Nothing downloads until you choose to start, so visiting this site never uses your data on its own. A device with WebGPU support is required.

Your browser doesn't support WebGPU, which this in-browser AI needs. Try the latest Chrome, Edge, or a Chromium-based browser on desktop. In the meantime, you can learn about Dan on the Experience, Projects, and Essays pages.
